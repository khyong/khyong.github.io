I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Visual & Geometric Intelligence (VGI) Lab](https://jaesik.info/) at Seoul National University, working with Prof. Jaesik Park. I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from [GIST](https://www.gist.ac.kr) in 2026, advised by Prof. Kangil Kim at the [IRR Lab](https://irrlab.github.io/).

My research asks **how deep networks organize information in their representation space**, and how that geometry can be understood, diagnosed, and shaped. My approach follows three steps: identify a model's failure from the perspective of representation learning, verify it theoretically and empirically, and propose a theory-grounded remedy.

During my Ph.D., I studied **Neural Collapse** and the collapse phenomena that degrade representations when idealized training assumptions break down. This line of work includes angle-only representations with the [Spherization Layer](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/67f30132d98e758f7b4e28c36091d86e-Abstract-Conference.html) (NeurIPS 2022), zero-mean Neural Collapse with a [fixed non-negative orthogonal classifier](https://openreview.net/forum?id=F4bmOrmUwc) (ICLR 2024), and [minority collapse caused by imbalanced mixed labels](https://openreview.net/forum?id=0g48FtWsjv) (NeurIPS 2026). With colleagues at the IRR Lab, I have also studied disentangled and object-centric representations (ICML 2026, TMLR 2026).

I am now extending this geometric view toward embodied AI. My current interests are:

- **Mechanistic interpretability** of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, using tools such as sparse autoencoders to study latent space geometry
- **World models and World Action Models** for robotics, with a focus on 3D geometry-aware representations
- **Representation learning theory**, including Neural Collapse and how representations align across modalities

See my [publications](/publications/), [research](/research/), and [CV](/cv/) for details.
