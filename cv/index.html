Professional Summary

Postdoctoral researcher at the Visual & Geometric Intelligence Lab, Seoul National University. I study how deep networks organize information in their representation space (Neural Collapse, representation collapse) and extend this geometric view to the interpretability of vision-language-action models and world models for robotics.
